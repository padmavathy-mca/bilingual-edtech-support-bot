# Mobile App

## How to install the mobile app
Search for our app on Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iPhone) and install it. Log in with the same account you use on the website. Your progress syncs automatically between devices.

## App crashes or does not open
Update the app to the latest version, restart your phone, and make sure you have at least 500 MB of free storage. If it still crashes, uninstall and reinstall the app. Your progress is saved to your account, so you will not lose it.

## Notifications are not coming
Open your phone settings, find the app, and allow notifications. Also check that Do Not Disturb mode is off and that notifications are enabled in the app under Profile and Settings.
