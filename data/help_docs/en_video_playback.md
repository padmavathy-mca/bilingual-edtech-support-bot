# Video Playback and Technical Issues

## Videos are buffering or not playing
Check your internet connection. A speed of at least 2 Mbps is recommended. Lower the video quality from the settings icon, close other tabs or apps that use the internet, and refresh the page.

## Supported browsers and devices
We recommend the latest version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. Update your browser if videos show a black screen. Turn off VPN or ad-blocking extensions, as they can block video.

## Still not working?
Send the support team your device, browser name, and a screenshot of the error, along with the course and lesson name. This helps them fix the problem faster.
