# Course Access

## How to access a course you purchased
After successful payment, your course appears under My Courses within 15 minutes. Log in with the same account you used to buy the course. If you used a different email while purchasing, log in with that email.

## How long do I have access to a course?
Self-paced courses are available for 12 months from the date of purchase. Live-batch courses are available until the batch ends, plus 3 months for recordings and materials.

## Course not showing after payment
Wait 15 minutes, refresh the page and log out and log in again. If the course is still missing, contact the support team with your payment confirmation email so they can check your order.
