# Payments and Invoices

## Accepted payment methods
We accept UPI, debit and credit cards, net banking and popular wallets. EMI is available on selected courses and is shown at checkout.

## How to download your invoice
Go to Profile, then Orders, select the purchase and choose Download Invoice. Invoices are available as PDF files. A copy is also emailed after every successful payment.

## Payment problems
For a failed payment, a double charge, or money deducted without course access, the billing team must review your case. The assistant cannot check or change payments, so it will connect you to a human agent and share a ticket summary so you do not have to repeat the details.
