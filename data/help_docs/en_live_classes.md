# Live Classes

## How to find your live class schedule
Open your dashboard and select Schedule. You will see upcoming live classes with the date, time (Indian Standard Time) and topic. You also receive an email reminder 24 hours before each class.

## How to join a live class
The Join button becomes active 10 minutes before the class starts. Click it on your dashboard or use the link in your reminder email. Use a laptop or desktop with Chrome for the best experience.

## What if I miss a live class or the class is rescheduled?
Every live class is recorded and added to your course within 24 hours. If a class is rescheduled, you will get an email and an app notification with the new time, and your dashboard schedule will update automatically.
