# Login Problems

## I cannot log in to my account
Check that you are using the email address or phone number you registered with, and that Caps Lock is off. Try another browser or a private window. If the problem continues, clear your browser cache and try again.

## How to reset your password
On the login page, select Forgot Password and enter your registered email. You will receive a reset link valid for 30 minutes. Check your spam folder if you do not see it.

## Safety reminder
Our support team and this assistant will never ask for your password or OTP. Never share them with anyone. If you think someone else has accessed your account, the assistant will connect you to a human agent immediately.
