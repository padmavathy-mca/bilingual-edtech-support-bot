# Course Certificates

## How to download your certificate
Your certificate is issued automatically within 7 days after you complete all required lessons and pass the final assessment. To download it, log in, go to My Courses, open the completed course, and select Download Certificate. The certificate is a PDF file.

## Why is my certificate not showing?
Check that you have completed 100% of the required lessons and scored at least the minimum passing mark of 60% in the final assessment. If you have met both conditions and more than 7 days have passed, contact the support team with your course name.

## Correcting the name on your certificate
The name on your certificate is taken from your profile at the time you complete the course. Update your profile name before completing the course. If your certificate is already issued, submit a name correction request through the support team.
