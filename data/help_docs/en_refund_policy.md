# Refund Policy

## Refund eligibility
You can request a refund within 7 days of purchase if you have completed less than 20% of the course content. Courses already completed, and certificates already issued, are not eligible for a refund. Sample values in this document are placeholders; replace them with your real policy.

## How refunds are processed
Refund requests are reviewed by our billing team. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days. Refund requests for a specific payment are handled only by the billing team, so the assistant will connect you to a human agent.

## Courses and offers that are not refundable
Discounted bundle purchases and special promotional offers may have different refund terms. These terms are shown on the checkout page at the time of purchase.
