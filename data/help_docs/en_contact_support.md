# Contacting Support

## How to reach a human agent
Select Talk to Support in the chat, or use Help and then Raise a Ticket in your dashboard. Include your registered email, course name and a short description of the problem.

## Support hours and response time
Human support is available Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 7 PM IST. Tickets are answered within 24 hours on working days. Billing, refund and account-security tickets are given priority.

## Support languages
Our support team helps you in English and Tamil. You can write to us in either language.
