# Class Recordings

## Where to find class recordings
Open your course and select the Recordings tab. Recordings of live classes are uploaded within 24 hours and are listed by date and topic.

## How long are recordings available?
Recordings stay available for the full duration of your course access. Recordings cannot be downloaded; they can only be watched online on the platform.

## Recording is missing or has no sound
If a recording is not available after 24 hours, or the audio is missing, report it using the Report a Problem button below the video, with the class date and topic. The team will re-upload it as soon as possible.
